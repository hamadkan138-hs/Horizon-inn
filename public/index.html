The Experience

Stillness, considered
in every detail.

Horizon Inn was built on a simple idea — that a guest house can hold the calm of a private retreat and the character of the city around it at once. Every room is dressed in warm, unfussy materials; every evening ends around the courtyard fire pit.

Ten minutes from Peshawar's old city, close enough to wander and far enough to rest, this is a stay designed for people who notice the details others skip.

Beyond The Courtyard

A city worth stepping
outside for.

Peshawar is one of South Asia's oldest living cities — and Horizon Inn sits close enough to reach its landmarks without losing the quiet of home.

01

Qissa Khwani Bazaar

The legendary "Bazaar of Storytellers" — centuries of trade, tea houses, and history in one narrow street.

02

Bala Hisar Fort

A commanding Mughal-era fortress overlooking the old city, still standing sentry after generations.

03

Peshawar Museum

One of Pakistan's finest collections of Gandharan art and frontier history, a short drive from the door.
